# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Northwind serves millions of retail customers and moves goods for thousands of business clients. That
trust depends on every one of our roughly 4,000 employees acting with honesty and respect. This Code
sets the minimum standard. It applies to all employees, officers, contractors, and temporary staff in
both business units.

## 1. Respect at work

- We do not tolerate harassment, bullying, or discrimination on any grounds.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Escalate conflict through your manager or People Operations rather
  than through public channels.
- Jokes or comments that a reasonable colleague would find demeaning are not acceptable, regardless of
  intent or of where they are made (office, warehouse floor, chat, video call).

## 2. Conflicts of interest

Disclose in PeopleHub (`Compliance > Disclosures`) within **10 working days** any situation where your
personal interests could conflict with Northwind's, including:

- A second job or directorship with a supplier, customer, or competitor.
- A close relative working for a supplier you select or manage.
- A financial interest above 5% in a company that does business with Northwind.

Disclosed conflicts are reviewed by Legal and Compliance, and most are resolved by recusal from the
relevant decisions.

## 3. Gifts, hospitality, and bribery

| Item                                       | Rule                                                        |
|--------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Gifts received from a business partner     | Allowed up to **100 USD** per source per year; declare above 50 USD |
| Gifts given to a business partner          | Allowed up to 100 USD per recipient per year, via Ledgerly  |
| Cash or cash equivalents (vouchers, crypto)| **Never** accepted or given                                 |
| Meals and events with partners             | Allowed if reasonable and business-related                  |
| Anything from a public official            | Requires Legal pre-approval regardless of value             |

Bribery and facilitation payments are prohibited everywhere we operate, even where local custom
tolerates them. If you are pressured to pay, refuse, leave if you can safely, and report it the same day.

## 4. Confidential information and data

Handle information according to the Data Classification Policy. Never share customer or employee
personal data outside Northwind without a documented legal basis. Do not use Northwind information for
personal gain, including trading on non-public information about Northwind or its partners.

## 5. Use of company systems

Northwind systems are for business use; reasonable personal use is accepted. Do not install unapproved
software, bypass security controls, or share credentials. Monitoring of systems is performed for
security and compliance purposes and is described in the IT Acceptable Use Standard.

## 6. Speaking up

If you see something that may breach this Code, the law, or Northwind policy, report it. You may:

1. Talk to your manager or any People Operations partner.
2. Email `ethics@northwind.example` (read only by Legal and Compliance).
3. Use the **Speak-Up line**, an independent, anonymous channel available 24/7 (details on the intranet
   under Compliance).

Every report is acknowledged within **5 business days**. Investigations are confidential and
proportionate. **Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a serious breach** and
leads to disciplinary action up to dismissal.

## 7. Consequences

Breaches are handled under the Disciplinary Procedure. Outcomes range from coaching to dismissal, and
some matters must be reported to authorities. Managers who ignore a reported concern are held to the same
standard as the person who caused it.

## 8. Acknowledgement

All employees confirm they have read this Code when joining and once a year in the annual compliance
training (due each **31 March**). Completion is tracked in PeopleHub.
