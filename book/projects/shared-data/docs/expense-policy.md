# Business Expense and Reimbursement Policy

## Principles

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable, necessary, and documented expenses incurred while doing
Northwind business. Spend as if it were your own money, keep receipts, and submit promptly. All claims
are submitted in **Ledgerly**, Northwind's expense system, and are paid with the next payroll run after
approval.

## Deadlines and approval thresholds

| Rule                                     | Value                                   |
|------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| Submission deadline                      | **Within 30 days** of the expense date  |
| Receipt required                         | Any single item **over 25 USD**         |
| Manager approval                         | Required for all claims                 |
| Second approval (Finance Operations)     | Claims **over 2,500 USD** in total      |
| Director approval                        | Any single item over 500 USD            |
| Reimbursement after final approval       | Within **10 business days**             |

Claims submitted more than 30 days after the expense date are rejected by default. Late claims up to
90 days may be accepted with a written justification approved by a Director. Nothing older than 90 days
is reimbursed.

## Meals and per diem

When travelling overnight, employees claim a per diem instead of itemised meal receipts:

- **Domestic travel: 65 USD per day.**
- **International travel: 95 USD per day.**
- Travel days (departure and return) are paid at 50% of the per diem.
- If a meal is provided (conference lunch, hotel breakfast included in the rate, client dinner paid by
  Northwind), reduce the per diem by 20% for breakfast, 30% for lunch, 50% for dinner.

Team meals that are not part of travel are reimbursed with itemised receipts up to 45 USD per person and
require the attendee list in the claim notes.

## Allowed and disallowed items

**Allowed**: flights and trains booked through TripDesk, hotels within the caps in the Travel Policy,
ground transport, conference fees approved in advance, home office items under the Remote Work stipend,
work-related books and online courses up to 300 USD per year, client gifts up to 100 USD per recipient
per year (see Code of Conduct).

**Not allowed**: alcohol outside client entertainment, minibar, in-flight Wi-Fi above 20 USD per flight,
traffic or parking fines, personal entertainment, upgrades not covered by the Travel Policy, expenses for
family members travelling with you, and any item for which a corporate card or purchase order should
have been used (software licences, hardware over 500 USD, consulting services).

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than 4 times a year may request a corporate card through their manager.
Card transactions still require a Ledgerly claim with receipts within 30 days. Personal use of the
corporate card is prohibited; accidental personal charges must be flagged in Ledgerly and repaid within
one payroll cycle.

## Mileage

Use of a personal car for business travel is reimbursed at the official national rate (currently
0.42 USD per km in the reference table in Ledgerly). Commuting between home and your assigned office is
not reimbursable. Attach the route (start, end, distance) to the claim.

## Currency and tax

Submit expenses in the currency on the receipt. Ledgerly converts at the daily rate of the expense date.
Enter the tax amount shown on the receipt separately when the receipt itemises it; this is needed for
VAT recovery.

## Audit

Finance Operations audits a random 5% of claims each month plus all claims over 2,500 USD. Employees are
asked to keep original receipts for 90 days after reimbursement. Repeated policy breaches are handled
under the Code of Conduct.

## Common rejections

1. Receipt missing for an item over 25 USD.
2. Submitted after the 30-day deadline without justification.
3. Hotel above the Travel Policy cap without pre-approval.
4. Per diem claimed together with itemised meal receipts for the same day.
5. Attendee list missing for a team meal.
