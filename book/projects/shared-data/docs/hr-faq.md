# HR Frequently Asked Questions

> **Note on currency of this page.** This FAQ (version 1.4) was last revised on 2025-06-10 and reflects
> the policies in force at that date. Policies are revised from time to time; where this page and a
> policy document disagree, the policy document applies. People Operations plans a full refresh of this
> FAQ in 2026.

## Time off

**How many days of PTO do I get?** 22 days per year for employees with less than 5 years' service,
25 days from 5 years, and 28 days from 10 years. Part-time staff accrue pro rata.

**How much PTO can I carry over into next year?** Under the PTO Policy in force since **1 January 2024**
(version 2.2), you may carry over **up to 5 unused days** into the next calendar year. Carried-over days
must be used by **31 March** or they are lost. There is no payout for forfeited days.

**How far in advance do I need to request PTO?** 14 calendar days for 5 or more consecutive working
days; 2 working days for shorter absences. Managers respond within 5 working days.

**Are there times when I cannot take PTO?** Retail has a blackout from 20 November to 31 December;
Logistics in the last 5 working days of each quarter. Director approval is needed in those windows.

**What happens to my PTO if I leave?** Up to 10 days of accrued, unused PTO is paid with your final
salary.

**I was sick during my holiday.** Provide a medical certificate within 5 working days of your return
and the days are converted to sick leave.

## Parental leave

**How long is parental leave?** 16 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the
secondary caregiver, after 6 months of service. Notify People Operations at least 8 weeks ahead.

**Can I split parental leave?** Yes, into up to two blocks within 12 months of the birth or placement.
The second block must be at least 2 weeks.

## Working arrangements

**How many days can I work from home?** Up to 3 days a week by default for office-based roles. Fully
remote needs Vice President approval.

**Can I work from another country for a few weeks?** Up to 20 working days per year, requested in
PeopleHub at least 10 working days ahead.

**What is the home office stipend?** 400 USD per year, claimed in Ledgerly with receipts.

## Pay and benefits

**When is payday?** Salaries are paid on the **25th** of each month, or the preceding business day if the
25th falls on a weekend or public holiday. Payslips appear in PeopleHub two business days before.

**How do I change my bank account?** In PeopleHub, `My Pay > Bank details`. Changes made before the
15th apply to that month's payroll. People Operations calls you back to confirm any bank change as a
fraud precaution.

**When are salary reviews?** Once a year, effective 1 April, communicated by managers in March.

**Does Northwind match pension contributions?** Yes, up to 6% of base salary when you contribute at
least 4%.

## Learning and development

**Is there a training budget?** Each employee has 1,200 USD per year for courses and conferences,
approved by the manager and booked through TripDesk or Ledgerly. Books and online courses up to 300 USD
per year can be expensed without using the budget.

## Leaving Northwind

**What is my notice period?** 1 month for most roles, 3 months for senior management; your contract is
authoritative.

**When is my access removed?** Within 24 hours of your last working day. Your mailbox is forwarded to
your manager for 30 days.

## Who to contact

- People Operations partner for your department: listed on the intranet `People > Who is my partner`.
- Beacon category `HR / General`: response within 2 business days.
- Urgent personal matters: the Employee Assistance Programme (24/7, confidential), details on the intranet.
