# Parental Leave Policy

## Who is eligible

All regular employees with at least **6 months of continuous service** at the expected date of birth,
adoption placement, or start of foster care are eligible for paid parental leave. Employees with less
than 6 months of service are entitled to the statutory minimum in their country of employment, which
People Operations will confirm in writing within 5 working days of the request.

## Entitlement

| Role                              | Paid leave at 100% salary |
|-----------------------------------|---------------------------|
| Primary caregiver                 | **16 weeks**              |
| Secondary caregiver               | **6 weeks**               |
| Multiple birth or adoption        | +2 weeks for either role  |

- "Primary caregiver" means the parent who takes the main responsibility for the child during the leave
  period. Parents who both work at Northwind choose who is primary; the choice is recorded in PeopleHub
  and may be changed once.
- Where statutory leave in the employee's country is longer or more generous, the statutory scheme
  applies and Northwind tops up pay to 100% for the durations above.
- Leave is paid at base salary. Variable pay (sales commission, on-call allowance) is paid at the
  average of the previous 6 months.

## Timing and splitting

- Leave may start up to **4 weeks before** the expected date and must begin within **12 months** of the
  birth or placement.
- Leave may be taken in **one or two blocks**. The second block must be at least 2 weeks long and must
  also fall within the 12-month window.
- Secondary caregivers may instead take their 6 weeks as a reduced schedule (for example 3 days per week
  for 15 weeks) if their manager agrees.

## How to request

1. Notify your manager and People Operations **at least 8 weeks before** the planned start date using
   the PeopleHub form `My Time > Parental leave`. Earlier notice is appreciated for planning, and shorter
   notice is accepted when the timing could not have been predicted (premature birth, short-notice
   placement).
2. Attach supporting documentation (medical confirmation of expected date, adoption or foster agency
   letter) within 4 weeks of the request. Documents are stored as Restricted data and are visible only to
   People Operations.
3. People Operations confirms the leave plan in writing within 5 working days.
4. Agree a handover plan with your manager at least 2 weeks before leave starts.

## During leave

- Benefits (health insurance, pension contributions, equipment) continue unchanged.
- PTO continues to accrue during paid parental leave.
- You are not expected to read email or attend meetings. Up to **5 optional "keeping in touch" days**
  may be worked during leave, by mutual agreement, paid as normal working days on top of leave pay.
- Access to Northwind systems is kept active but your manager may set your status to "On leave" so that
  tickets and approvals are rerouted.

## Returning to work

- You return to the same role, or an equivalent role with the same pay grade, location flexibility and
  seniority.
- A phased return (reduced hours for up to 4 weeks at full pay) may be agreed with your manager.
- Employees returning from a primary caregiver leave are exempt from the next performance calibration
  round's distribution targets; their rating is based on the period actually worked.

## Pregnancy loss and bereavement

Employees who experience pregnancy loss at any stage, or the death of a child, are entitled to 10 days
of paid compassionate leave for either parent, with no documentation required. Further leave is handled
case by case with People Operations.
