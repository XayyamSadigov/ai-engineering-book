# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Effective date: 2026-01-01.** This version (3.0) supersedes PTO Policy 2.2 (effective 2024-01-01)
and any older guidance, including HR FAQ entries published before 2026. Where this document and an
older FAQ disagree, this document wins.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees of Northwind in both business
units (Retail and Logistics). Contractors and agency staff are covered by their own agreements and are
out of scope. Questions go to People Operations through the Beacon portal (category `HR / Time off`).

## 2. Annual entitlement

| Tenure at 1 January | Annual PTO (full-time) |
|---------------------|------------------------|
| Less than 5 years   | 22 days                |
| 5 to 9 years        | 25 days                |
| 10 years or more    | 28 days                |

- PTO accrues monthly at 1/12 of the annual entitlement (for 22 days that is about 1.83 days per month).
- Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata to their contracted hours.
- Public holidays observed by Northwind are separate and are not deducted from PTO.
- New hires start accruing on their first day; they may go up to 3 days negative in their first 6 months
  with manager approval.

## 3. Carryover (changed in this version)

**From 1 January 2026, employees may carry over up to 10 unused PTO days into the next calendar year.**
The previous limit under Policy 2.2 was 5 days. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March** of the
following year or they are forfeited. There is no cash payout for forfeited carryover days.

Example: an employee with 22 days of entitlement who has used 9 days by 31 December 2026 has 13 unused
days. 10 days carry into 2027 and must be used by 31 March 2027; the remaining 3 days are forfeited.

## 4. Requesting time off

1. Submit the request in PeopleHub (`My Time > Request PTO`) **at least 14 calendar days in advance** for
   absences of 5 or more consecutive working days. Shorter absences should be requested at least 2 working
   days in advance.
2. Managers must approve or decline within 5 working days. A request not answered in 5 working days is
   escalated to the manager's manager automatically.
3. Approved PTO appears in the team calendar and the on-call roster. Employees on an on-call rotation are
   responsible for arranging a swap before taking PTO.
4. Emergency time off (for example a family emergency) may be reported after the fact and recorded as
   PTO within 3 working days.

## 5. Blackout periods

- **Retail**: 20 November to 31 December. Requests in this window need director approval.
- **Logistics**: the last 5 working days of each quarter (quarter-end close). Requests need director
  approval.

## 6. Payout on leaving Northwind

Unused accrued PTO up to a maximum of 10 days is paid out with the final salary. Days above 10, and any
unused carryover, are not paid out. Negative balances are deducted from the final pay.

## 7. Sickness

Sick leave is separate from PTO and is covered by the Sick Leave Guidelines (HR-GL-07). Sick days are not
deducted from PTO. If an employee falls ill during approved PTO and provides a medical certificate within
5 working days of returning, the affected days are reclassified as sick leave.
