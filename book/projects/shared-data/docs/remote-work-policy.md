# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Summary

Northwind operates a hybrid model. Most office-based roles may work remotely **up to 3 days per week**
by default, with the remaining days in a Northwind office or site. Fully remote arrangements require
written approval from a Vice President and a review every 12 months. Roles that are inherently on-site
(store staff, warehouse operations, drivers, facilities) are not eligible for remote work; this is
stated in the role's job description.

## Eligibility

| Arrangement            | Default availability        | Approval needed                 |
|------------------------|-----------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Hybrid (up to 3 days)  | All office-based roles      | Manager (recorded in PeopleHub) |
| Hybrid (4 days)        | By exception                | Director                        |
| Fully remote           | By exception                | Vice President                  |
| Remote from abroad     | Up to 20 working days/year  | Manager + People Operations     |

Remote work must take place in the employee's **country of employment**, except for the short-term
"work from abroad" allowance of up to 20 working days per calendar year, which must be requested in
PeopleHub at least 10 working days in advance so that tax and insurance implications can be checked.

## Core hours

Teams agree their own working patterns, but everyone in a hybrid or remote arrangement must be reachable
during **core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 local time** on working days. Outside core hours, asynchronous
communication is the default. Managers may not require camera-on attendance outside core hours.

## Equipment and stipend

- Every eligible employee receives a laptop, headset, and (on request) an external monitor from IT.
  See the Laptop Replacement Runbook for refresh cycles.
- A **home office stipend of 400 USD per calendar year** (or local equivalent) covers desk, chair,
  lighting, or connectivity. Claims are submitted through Ledgerly under the category `Home office`
  and follow the Expense Policy (receipts required, submission within 30 days).
- Unused stipend does not roll over.
- Northwind does not reimburse home internet subscriptions or utility costs separately; these are
  covered by the stipend.

## Security requirements

1. Work only on Northwind-managed devices. Personal devices may access email and chat through the
   managed browser profile only.
2. Connect to internal systems through NorthGate VPN (see the VPN Access Runbook). Public Wi-Fi is
   allowed only with the VPN active.
3. Lock your screen when stepping away. Devices auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity; this setting
   cannot be changed by the user.
4. Do not print Confidential or Restricted documents at home (see the Data Classification Policy).
5. Take calls involving customer or employee data in a private space.

## Office attendance

Each team designates at least **one anchor day per week** when all team members are expected in the
office. Anchor days are published in the team calendar. Employees who live more than 80 km from their
assigned office may request a reduced attendance expectation from their Director.

## Changing or ending an arrangement

Either the employee or Northwind can propose a change to a remote arrangement with **4 weeks' notice**.
Northwind may end a fully remote arrangement with 8 weeks' notice if business needs change. Performance
concerns are handled through the normal performance process and are not, on their own, a reason to
revoke hybrid eligibility.

## Frequently misunderstood points

- Hybrid days are not accrued; you cannot "save" remote days for a later week.
- Working remotely while on PTO is not permitted; PTO means not working.
- Managers may set team-level anchor days but may not reduce the default below 3 remote days per week
  without a Director-approved exception recorded in PeopleHub.
