# Business Travel Policy

## Booking

All business travel is booked through **TripDesk**, Northwind's travel portal, which applies this policy
automatically and holds the negotiated rates. Bookings outside TripDesk are reimbursed only if TripDesk
was unavailable and the Service Desk ticket number for the outage is included in the expense claim.

- Book flights and trains **at least 14 days before departure**. Bookings inside 14 days require a
  reason code in TripDesk and are reported to the traveller's Director monthly.
- Trips that are expected to exceed **3,000 USD** in total need Director approval in TripDesk before
  booking.
- Travel involving more than 3 Northwind employees on the same flight requires a risk check by Security
  (TripDesk prompts for it).

## Air travel class

| Flight duration (scheduled, per segment) | Class allowed     |
|------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| Under 6 hours                            | Economy           |
| 6 hours or more                          | Premium economy   |
| Overnight flights of 8 hours or more     | Business, with Director approval |

Loyalty points earned on business travel may be kept by the traveller. Paid upgrades are at the
traveller's own expense and are not reimbursed.

## Rail

Rail is preferred over flying for journeys under 4 hours door to door. Standard class is the default;
first class is allowed when its price is within 20% of the standard fare or when working on the train is
required and no quiet standard-class seat is available.

## Accommodation

| Location                     | Nightly cap (room rate before tax) |
|------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Domestic                     | **220 USD**                        |
| International                | **300 USD**                        |
| Listed high-cost cities      | 380 USD (list maintained in TripDesk) |

Choose hotels from TripDesk's preferred list where available. If no preferred hotel within the cap is
available, TripDesk records the exception automatically. Booking a private rental apartment is allowed
for stays of 5 nights or more when it is cheaper than the hotel cap.

## Ground transport

- Use public transport, shared rides, or taxis as appropriate for safety and time of day.
- Rental cars: **intermediate class** or smaller; larger vehicles only when carrying equipment or 3 or
  more passengers. Decline the rental company's insurance: Northwind's corporate travel insurance covers
  rental damage.
- Mileage for personal cars follows the Expense Policy.

## Insurance and safety

Every TripDesk booking automatically enrols the traveller in Northwind's travel insurance and the
traveller-tracking service used by Security during emergencies. Keep the emergency card (in the TripDesk
confirmation email) with you. Destinations rated "High risk" in TripDesk require Security approval and a
briefing before booking.

## Per diem and expenses

Meals on overnight trips are covered by the per diem in the Expense Policy (65 USD domestic, 95 USD
international). Submit all travel expenses in Ledgerly within 30 days of the return date.

## Combining business and personal travel

Personal days may be added to a business trip if the total cost to Northwind does not increase. Attach a
TripDesk price comparison for the business-only itinerary. Northwind's insurance does not cover the
personal portion.

## Cancellations

Cancel in TripDesk as soon as plans change. Non-refundable costs from late cancellation are covered by
Northwind when the cancellation was for business reasons or illness; repeated avoidable losses are
reviewed with the traveller's manager.
