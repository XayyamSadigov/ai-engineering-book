Dear Northwind team,

Thank you for another strong quarter together. Brightline Supply Co. has been the packaging and
consumables partner for Northwind Logistics' depots since 2022, and we are pleased to share what is new
this spring.

## New in the catalogue

| Item                          | SKU          | Change                                   |
|-------------------------------|--------------|------------------------------------------|
| Recycled corrugated box, M    | BL-BOX-M-R   | Price reduced 4% from 1 May 2026         |
| Thermal label roll 100x150    | BL-LBL-1015  | New 1,000-label core compatible with PRN-PACK printers |
| Chilled liner, insulated      | BL-CHL-L     | Now certified for 12-hour cold chain     |
| Tote lid, stackable           | BL-TOTE-LID  | Replaces BL-TOTE-LID-OLD, same dimensions |

Orders placed through the usual purchase order process are unaffected; please reference your PO number
on every order so our team can match deliveries to Northwind's three-way check.

## Delivery schedule changes

From June 2026, deliveries to depots in the North region move from Tuesdays to Wednesdays to align with
our regional hub. The South and Central schedules are unchanged. Depot managers will receive a calendar
invitation from their Brightline account representative.

## Sustainability update

Our recycled corrugated range now makes up 71% of the volume we ship to Northwind, up from 58% a year
ago. We will publish a joint sustainability statement with Northwind Procurement in the autumn.

## Account team

Your account team remains unchanged. For order questions, use the vendor portal ticketing system; for
contract matters, contact your Northwind Procurement category manager, who will coordinate with us.

## Upcoming events

- **Packaging innovation webinar**: 14 May 2026, 10:00 CET, registration via the vendor portal.
- **Depot visit programme**: our engineers will visit six Northwind depots between May and July to review
  packing station ergonomics. Dates are agreed with depot managers individually.

## Invoicing reminder

Please note that from 1 July 2026 all Brightline invoices will carry a QR code for faster matching. The
invoice numbering format `BL-INV-YYYY-NNNNN` is unchanged. Payment terms remain net 30 days from the
invoice date. Queries about invoices should go to our accounts team through the vendor portal, not to
individual Northwind employees.

Thank you for your continued partnership.

The Brightline Supply Co. team
